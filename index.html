The food took quite a while, but it was mostly because of a family that for some reason ordered 11 soda cups(?) When finally i get my food (I’m attaching photos below) i was dissapointed bc the burger is sooooooo small, but... delicious, the chicken is so cripsy and i like it. The potatoes was good, but its so small too. (everithing is small now?), but anyways. The empanadas were really good, have so much cheese and salt. Simply perfect. I didn't want soda and asked for a natural juice, but for some reason they only had orange, but the juice was good, fortunately i like orange. My experience in Tarragona in general was... good but not perfect, i repeat. I give Tarragona an 8/10 stars!
